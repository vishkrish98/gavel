STUDENT GOVERNMENT FUNDING POLICY (SYNTHETIC, FOR SOFTWARE TESTING ONLY)

1.1  The Student Government Funding Committee (the Committee) reviews club budget requests each semester. This policy is synthetic and written for software testing only.
1.2  Clubs are encouraged to co-sponsor events with other clubs.
2.1  The Committee may consider a club's prior-year utilization when allocating funds.
3.1  Funds may not be used to purchase alcohol.
3.2  Funds may not be used to purchase gift cards.
3.3  Prizes and giveaways may not exceed $25 per item.
3.4  Food and beverages are funded up to $15 per person per event.
3.5  Any single purchase over $500 requires three written quotes.
3.6  Speaker fees are funded up to $1,500 per event and require a signed contract.
3.7  Lodging is funded up to $150 per room per night.
3.8  Club apparel is funded up to $20 per item.
3.9  Equipment with a unit cost over $1,000 is not funded.
4.1  Requests must be submitted at least 14 days before the event.
4.2  Purchases made before the Committee approves a request are not reimbursed.
4.3  No club may receive more than $8,000 in a semester.
5.1  Questions about this policy may be directed to the Committee Treasurer.
